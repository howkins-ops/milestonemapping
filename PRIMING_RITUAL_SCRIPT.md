# Prime Your State — Narration Script (v1 draft)

**What this is:** the app's own *Get Grateful & Visualize* — a voice-guided morning priming ritual,
sales/entrepreneur-minded. Lives as a **daily ritual card** on the Morning page. Press play, close
your eyes, get primed. Modeled on the *structure* of Tony Robbins' Hour of Power, written in the
Milestone Mapping voice (built-from-nothing, door-to-door, grit → breakthrough).

**Voice direction:** male, warm but charged. A coach who's been in the trenches, not a guru.
Grounded low energy at the start, rising to fired-up by the end. Speaks in **present tense**, second
person ("you"). Never cheesy. Leaves real silence on the `[PAUSE]` cues — that's where the work happens.

**Runtime:** ~9 min full. Segments are self-contained so we can also ship a **Quick 5** (Arrive +
Grateful + Visualize the Day) and a **Full 9**. Durations are spoken-time + pause-time.

**On-screen (VISUAL) notes** describe what the card shows while each segment plays — this is where the
listener's *real* data (Top Five, next milestones, projects) appears as visual anchors.

**Recording note:** `[PAUSE Ns]` = leave N seconds of silence (or soft bed music only). `[BREATH]` =
audible cue/chime for the listener to breathe with. Bracketed lines are not spoken.

---

## SEGMENT 1 — ARRIVE + BREATHE  (~90s)
*(VISUAL: dim screen, a single slow-pulsing orb that expands on inhale, contracts on exhale. No data yet.)*

Wherever you are right now — sit up tall. Both feet down. Let the phone rest in your hand.

This is the most important sixty seconds of your day. Not because of what you'll do — because of who
you're about to become before you do anything.

Most people wake up and grab their day by the tail — reacting, scrolling, bracing. Not you. Not today.
Today you prime. You set the state first, and you let the day rise to meet it.

Let's breathe. In through the nose — fill all the way up.

[BREATH] [PAUSE 3s]

And out. Let the shoulders drop.

[BREATH] [PAUSE 3s]

Again — in. Bigger. Pull the energy up from the floor.

[BREATH] [PAUSE 3s]

And out. Everything you were carrying from yesterday — let it go. That chapter's closed.

[BREATH] [PAUSE 3s]

One more. In — this is you turning the engine on.

[BREATH] [PAUSE 4s]

And out. Good. You're here now. Fully here.

[PAUSE 2s]

## SEGMENT 2 — GET GRATEFUL  (~2 min)
*(VISUAL: warm gold light blooms. Three soft "slots" fade in one at a time as the narration names them. If the user already logged gratitude today, their entries can glow faintly in the background.)*

Before we build the day — let's fill the tank. Gratitude isn't soft. Gratitude is state control. You
cannot be grateful and afraid at the same time. You cannot be grateful and desperate. And a person who
walks into the day full instead of empty — that's the person who closes, who leads, who wins.

So let's find three. And we're not going to *list* them. We're going to *feel* them.

**First — something small.** Something you'd normally walk right past. The warmth of the room. Coffee.
That you woke up at all. Pick one tiny thing right now — and actually let it land.

[PAUSE 6s]

Feel that. That's real. That was always there — you just looked at it for once.

[PAUSE 2s]

**Second — a person.** Someone who's in your corner. Someone who believed you when you had nothing to
show yet. Put their face in front of you right now. See them.

[PAUSE 6s]

If they could feel what you feel for them right now — they would. Sit in that for a second.

[PAUSE 3s]

**Third — something about you.** Not what you've got — what you've *survived*. The grit it took to get
to this exact morning. Every hard thing that tried to break you and didn't. That's not luck. That's you.

[PAUSE 6s]

Full tank. Now we point it at the day.

[PAUSE 2s]

---

## SEGMENT 3 — VISUALIZE THE DAY  (~2 min)
*(VISUAL: the card fades in TODAY'S TOP FIVE — the user's real priorities — as a checklist. As the
narration moves through the day, each item lights up and gets a ✓, one by one, "already done.")*

Now we run the day — before the day runs you. Your mind can't tell the difference between something
vividly imagined and something real. So let's give it a memory of a day that hasn't happened yet.

See yourself, twelve hours from now, at the end of today — looking back. And it was a *great* day.

Watch it. You start strong — no snooze, no drift. You hit the first hard thing early, the one you'd
normally avoid, and you *move* on it while everyone else is still waking up.

[PAUSE 4s]

See the work you set out to do today — see it getting done. One by one.

[PAUSE 4s]
*(VISUAL: Top Five items check off here, ✓ ✓ ✓)*

And see how you carry yourself while you do it. Calm. Certain. Energy up. When you talk to people today
— on the phone, at the door, across the table — you lead with that energy *before* you lead with words.
People feel it. They lean in.

[PAUSE 4s]

See the conversation you've been avoiding — and see yourself having it anyway. Clean. Direct. Handled.

[PAUSE 4s]

Every "no" today doesn't shrink you. It aims you. One step closer. See yourself staying in the game,
unattached, present, all the way to the last rep.

[PAUSE 3s]

That day is available to you. You just watched it. Now go take it.

[PAUSE 2s]

---

## SEGMENT 4 — VISUALIZE YOUR PROJECTS COMPLETE  (~2.5 min)
*(VISUAL: the card pulls the user's active PROJECTS and their NEXT MILESTONE. Each project appears as a
card; as narration lands, the next milestone flips to "COMPLETE" with a small celebration burst. If a
project has a `futureVision`, it surfaces as a caption. Falls back to a generic goal prompt if none set.)*

The day is fuel. But you're not here for a good day. You're here for the *life*. So let's go past today
and see the finish line — and let's stand on it.

Bring up the thing you're building. The project. The goal with your name on it. See it — not started,
not halfway — **done.**

[PAUSE 5s]
*(VISUAL: user's #1 project + its next milestone flips to COMPLETE ✓)*

See the milestone you're on right now — the very next one — already behind you. Crossed off. See the
proof of it, real and solid in front of you.

[PAUSE 5s]

Now feel the moment it lands. The exhale. The quiet "I did it." Maybe someone you love is right there.
Maybe it's just you and the truth that you kept your word to yourself this time. Feel it in your body —
the win, the weight lifting, the pride. Let it be real for three whole seconds.

[PAUSE 5s]

Because here's the secret the pros know: you don't wait until you win to feel like that. You feel like
that *first* — and it pulls you forward. That feeling is not the reward at the end. It's the fuel at
the start.

[PAUSE 3s]

Look at what you're building.
*(VISUAL: remaining projects / vision-board images pan across.)*
This isn't a fantasy. It's a set of coordinates. And every one of them gets closer the second you get
up from this.

[PAUSE 3s]

---

## SEGMENT 5 — DECLARE + SEAL  (~1 min)
*(VISUAL: screen sharpens to full brightness, accent color intensifies. A single line of the user's own
declaration can appear, or hand off to the Daily Stand.)*

One thing left. You've felt the gratitude. You've seen the day. You've stood on the finish line. Now
you *declare* who's walking out that door.

Say it with me — in your head or out loud, but *mean* it:

**I am my word.** [PAUSE 2s]
**I show up before I feel ready.** [PAUSE 2s]
**I create value everywhere I go.** [PAUSE 2s]
**Today, I close the gap between who I am and who I'm becoming.** [PAUSE 3s]

That's not a hope. That's a decision. And you just made it.

[PAUSE 2s]

---

## SEGMENT 6 — LAUNCH  (~30s)
*(VISUAL: burst / sunrise animation. "PRIMED." stamp. XP awarded. CTA: "Go execute →")*

You're primed. Full tank, clear day, finish line in sight, and an identity you chose on purpose.

The version of you that just showed up for the next nine minutes? Keep him. Take him into the first
call, the first door, the first hard thing.

Now stop listening. Stand up.

And go build the milestone.

[PAUSE 1s]

*(End. Chime. Card flips to its "PRIMED — locked in" state.)*

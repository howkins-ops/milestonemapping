# MAPQUEST CITY — The Bible

*The living open-world hub of Milestone Mapping. One city, sixteen districts, every district a real feature. Master concept, canon, and design law.*

---

## The One Law

**Real life first. Always.**

The city only ever *reflects* real action. XP is evidence. Glow is proof. Nothing prestigious in this world can be earned by screen time, grinding, or purchase — the most impressive things a citizen can display were all paid for with kept promises in the real world.

Corollaries:
- A building lights up because you did the work its district represents. Never because you tapped it enough.
- Every lesson, quest, and mentor interaction ends by sending you into a real exercise. **The lesson ends where the work begins.**
- If a mechanic would make someone open the app *instead of* living their life, it's cut.

## The Fantasy

You don't open a planner. You log into the future version of yourself.

The city is what your inner world would look like if it were a place: your discipline is infrastructure, your streaks are fire, your shadow work is an under-city, your people are the population. A new citizen arrives to scaffolding and work-lights — untapped potential. A Legend walks under aurora skies with the Spire beacon burning. Same city. Different citizen.

## The World

**MapQuest City** is a cyberpunk-mystic metropolis rendered in the house palette (neon on deep black, warm and inviting, never horror). It lives inside **The Accountability Zone** — the Zone is the city's population and front door — and evolves along two axes:

- **Evolution stages (rank-driven):** Foundations → Rising Grid → Neon Ascent → Thriving Metropolis. Mapped from the 7 XP ranks (Starter → Legend). Stage only changes atmosphere and density — every district exists at every stage.
- **Time of day (real clock):** dawn / day / dusk / night sky palettes from the citizen's actual local time.

## The Mentors

Every district has a resident NPC teacher with a name, an epithet, rotating ambient greetings, and one real lesson (3–5 typed-dialogue beats) transmuted from the coaching IP. The Alchemist himself keeps the Spire. **The Guide** stands in the plaza and points every citizen at one district per day.

Rules of the cast:
- Lessons teach one gem each (survival mechanism vs. essence, declarations and stands, insight→action→results, completion, problems-as-opportunities…). Transmuted, never transcribed.
- A mentor lesson pays a small XP honorarium once per mentor per day — then hands you the real exercise CTA. The mentor never replaces the work.

## Citizen Identity

The **Citizen Card** is the player's face in the world, fused from systems that already exist:
- **Rank + XP** (7 ranks, Starter → Legend) with the rank sigil tinted by tier.
- **Zone identity**: @username, avatar, identity title, fire tier (cold → phoenix), phoenix stage (egg → legendary).
- **Derived titles**, earned only by evidence: Shadow Alchemist, Forgemaster, Signal Bearer, Vault Opener, Essence Collector, phoenix-stage epithets. No title is purchasable.

## The Social Fabric

The Zone *is* the city's society — nothing is duplicated, everything is surfaced:
- **Friends** → avatar chips standing in the Plaza, each wrapped in their real fire-tier aura.
- **Squads** → guilds, with banners in the Plaza (rooftop district banners in Phase 3).
- **Partner** → your ally chip.
- **az_challenges** → the Arena (Phase 3): 1v1s, squad wars, seasons.
- **az_get_leaderboard** → the Hall of Champions: podium + consistency board. Celebration, not shame — only those who showed up are ranked; misses are never displayed.
- **The Witness** remains the Zone's voice; the city's mentors are its teachers.

## The Psychology Loop

Real action → progress → recognition → identity → motivation → more real action.

The city's job is the *recognition* and *identity* links: making invisible consistency visible (glow, fire, titles, the Hall) and letting the citizen watch themselves become someone (stages, phoenix, the evolving skyline).

## What We Will Never Build

- Pay-to-win. Anything competitive is earned only.
- Fake grinding — no mechanic that rewards time-in-app itself.
- Shame mechanics — no red streaks, no public misses, no loss-aversion traps.
- Dark patterns — no manufactured FOMO, no manipulative notifications.
- Casino mechanics — no loot boxes, no gambling loops.

## Voice

Cinematic but concrete. Second person. The city speaks like the Witness and the Alchemist: warm, direct, a little mythic, zero corporate cheese. Fonts are Sora and Manrope only. The palette is the brand's six neons on deep black — "no white panels, ever."

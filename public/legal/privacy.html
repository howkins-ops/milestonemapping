Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Last updated: July 26, 2026

Milestone Mapping ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal-development app for adults. It combines goal and milestone mapping, daily planning, journaling, a fitness and nutrition mode, self-development exercises, a substance and behaviour recovery program (CLEARDAY), games, and a private social space (the Accountability Zone).

This policy explains what we collect, why, who it goes to, how long we keep it, and what you can make us do about it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we run no ads, no analytics SDKs and no trackers, we never sell or share your personal information, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.

Sensitive data notice. Depending on which features you use, this app handles information about your health, fitness, mental wellbeing, substance and behaviour recovery, and religious beliefs. That is described in detail below. We treat it as sensitive, we do not use it for advertising, and we never sell it.

1 · Who is responsible

Milestone Mapping is operated by Jon Howkins as an individual developer. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact howkins.ops@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days, and within any shorter period the law requires.

2 · What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash — we never see your password).
  • A user ID assigned to your account, plus the username, display name, identity title and avatar you choose in the Accountability Zone.

Content you create

  • Milestones, projects, goals, daily plans, weekly reviews, vision boards, identity statements and rewards.
  • Zone content: posts, missions, proof photos, captions, comments, reactions, squad and challenge activity, and direct messages.
  • Field Journal entries across five pillars — physical, intellectual, emotional, spiritual and financial — including mood notes and the Prayer Ledger. Prayer entries reveal religious belief and are treated as sensitive information.

Health, fitness and wellbeing data

  • Fitness (THE IRON, Alpha Mode) — workout plans and sessions, exercises, sets, reps and weights lifted, personal records, and the body weight and body-fat estimate you enter.
  • Nutrition (Alpha Mode) — calorie and macro targets, meal, fasting, cardio and cheat-day logs, and any meal photos you add to the Stockpile.
  • Wellbeing exercises — mood check-ins and completion records from the Anger Gym, Shadow Work, Anxiety SOS, Fill Your Cup and similar exercises.
  • CLEARDAY recovery data — if you use CLEARDAY: which tracks you chose (cannabis and/or pornography), your identity claim and personal rules, your named triggers and reasons, your daily clear-or-slip record, urge-battle logs (intensity, risk level, outcome), nightly written inventories, repair notes, and the signature image you draw when you sign a daily contract. This is health data about substance and behaviour use, and it is among the most sensitive information the app holds.

Progress and device data

  • Game progress, streaks, XP, ranks, achievements and feature state. Much of this lives only on your device; if you are signed in it also syncs to your account so you don't lose it.

What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no tracking of any kind. There is no ad identifier, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and therefore no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No contacts, no location, no background microphone access. The camera and photo library are used only at the moment you choose to attach a photo.
  • No payment information. The app is free and contains no purchases.

3 · Using the app without an account

You can tap "Continue without an account" and use almost the entire app — milestones, daily planning, journaling, fitness, CLEARDAY, exercises and games — with everything stored only on your device. Only the Accountability Zone, which is inherently social, requires an account.

5 · Who can see your content

The Accountability Zone is a private social space. Your posts, proofs and profile are visible only to members you have connected with — friends, squad members, accountability partners or fellow challenge participants — according to the app's visibility rules, which are enforced in the database itself. Direct messages are visible only to the people in that conversation. Blocking a member cuts visibility in both directions.

Nothing you create is listed, indexed or searchable on the open web. A few images you generate or attach — vision-board and reward images — are stored at private, unguessable web addresses so they load quickly; they are not discoverable, but anyone you deliberately send such a link to could open that single image.

CLEARDAY urge-battle proof photos never leave your device. They are compressed, stripped of embedded metadata and stored in your phone's private app storage. They are never uploaded and never posted to the Zone.

7 · AI features, and the consent we ask for

Some features send text to a third-party AI company. None of them run in the background, and none of them send anything until you have specifically agreed, by provider, in a screen that names that provider. You can decline and keep using the app; you can withdraw consent later in Settings.

THE CORNER and THE CLAIM FORGE (CLEARDAY) — Anthropic

THE CORNER is an AI chat inside CLEARDAY. THE CLAIM FORGE turns writing you have done into candidate identity statements. Both send data to Anthropic, the company behind Claude, and both ask for your consent first.

  • What is sent: the messages or paragraphs you type, plus limited context — which track you are on (cannabis and/or pornography), your day count, your identity claim, your current rule and the name you gave your "Mask".
  • What is never sent: your name, email, user ID, photos, journal entries, prayers, messages, workout data, or anything else in the app.
  • Anthropic processes the request and returns a reply. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, this content is not used to train their models.
  • We do not keep a server-side copy of these conversations; the chat lives in the app while it is open.
  • THE CORNER is an AI, not a person. It is not a therapist, counselor, sponsor, doctor or crisis line.

Vision board and reward images — Pollinations.ai

When you generate an image, the prompt text you type is sent to Pollinations.ai and an image comes back. Only your prompt is shared — no account details and no other content. Please don't put sensitive personal details in image prompts.

Spoken audio

Most voice in the app is pre-recorded and ships inside the app. Where audio is generated live, only the short scripted line of text is sent to ElevenLabs (or, as a fallback, Pollinations.ai). Nothing about you is attached.

10 · Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all server-side data yourself, inside the app: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & All Data. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — profile, posts, messages, journal, prayers, workouts, recovery data, proofs and uploaded media are all erased, and your login stops working.

You can also request deletion without opening the app, at milestonemapping.netlify.app/legal/delete-account.html, or by emailing us.

11 · Your rights

Wherever you live, you can email us to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to withdraw a consent you gave. We will not treat you differently for exercising a right.

If you are in the UK, EEA or Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the features you asked for — never to infer characteristics about you. There is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link because there is nothing to opt out of.

Other US states and Canada

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and other states with comparable laws, and residents of Canada under PIPEDA, have equivalent rights. Email us and we will honour them.

12 · Age requirement

Milestone Mapping is for adults aged 18 and over. The app contains frequent strong language, cartoon violence, and frank adult discussion of substance and pornography use. It is not directed at children, and it is rated accordingly on the App Store and Google Play.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 within the meaning of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that a child has created an account, we will delete it and the associated data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email us and we will remove it.

13 · Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encrypted at rest by our database provider. Access to your rows is enforced at the database level, so one account cannot read another's data. Secret keys for AI and voice services are held on our servers and never shipped inside the app. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

14 · A note on wellbeing and health content

Milestone Mapping includes fitness programs, nutrition calculators, self-development exercises and a substance and behaviour recovery program. It is a self-help and training tool. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis or treatment from a licensed physician or mental-health professional. See our Health Disclaimer.

If you are in crisis: call your local emergency number, or in the US call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For substance use in the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357.

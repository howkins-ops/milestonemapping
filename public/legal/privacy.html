Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Milestone Mapping ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal-development and accountability app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the control you have over it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we show you no ads and run no third-party trackers.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password (stored as a secure hash), and the username and profile details you choose inside the Accountability Zone.
  • Content you create — milestones, goals, declarations, posts, comments, direct messages, journal entries (including mood check-ins and, if you use them, gratitude and prayer entries), proof photos, and other content you save or share in the app.
  • Wellbeing exercise data — completion records from self-development exercises (for example anger-gym sessions, anxiety wave rides, and shadow-work reflections). Most of this stays on your device; entries you explicitly save to your journal or share to the Zone are stored with your account.
  • Progress data — game progress, streaks, XP, achievements, and feature completion, stored on your device and/or your account so your journey syncs.
  • Diagnostics we do NOT collect — we run no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking. We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or camera roll except when you actively choose to attach a photo.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sync your progress, show your posts to the people you have connected with, and deliver messages and notifications you have asked for.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing content that other members report.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Ever.

Who can see your content

The Accountability Zone is a private social space. Your posts, proofs, and profile are visible only to members you connect with (friends, squad members, accountability partners, or challenge participants) according to the app's visibility rules. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Nothing you create is public on the open web.

Where your data lives

Account data and content are stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) on infrastructure in the United States. The app's web version is served by Netlify. Both act as processors on our behalf and do not use your data for their own purposes. Some progress data is stored only on your device.

Optional AI image & voice features

Two optional creative features use Pollinations.ai, a third-party AI service: generating an image for a vision board or reward from a text prompt you write, and (as a fallback) generating spoken audio for certain guided scripts. When you use image generation, the prompt text you type is sent to Pollinations.ai to create the image. We recommend not putting sensitive personal details in image prompts. These features never run in the background — nothing is sent unless you actively use them — and no other personal data (not your journal, messages, or account details) is ever shared with Pollinations.ai.

How long we keep it

Your data stays in your account until you delete it — either individual items (deleting a post, message, or proof) or everything at once (below). Content removed for breaking our community rules may be retained briefly in moderation records.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all server-side data at any time, inside the app: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & All Data. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — your profile, posts, messages, journal, proofs, and uploaded media are erased. You can also email us (below) and we will delete your account for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal information. Email us and we will honor them.

Age requirement

Milestone Mapping is intended for adults and is not directed at children. Some content in the app is intended for mature audiences. Do not use the app if you are under the age indicated in the App Store listing.

A note on wellbeing content

Milestone Mapping includes self-development exercises for topics like anger, stress, and personal growth. It is a self-help tool, not medical care, and is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a licensed physician or mental-health professional.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we will update this page and note the new date at the top.

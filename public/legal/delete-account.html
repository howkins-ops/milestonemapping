Delete Your Account and Data

Milestone Mapping · Last updated: July 26, 2026

You can permanently delete your Milestone Mapping account and everything stored with it. There are two ways to do it, and you do not need to reinstall the app to use the second one.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (instant)

  1. Open Milestone Mapping and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete Account & All Data.
  5. Type DELETE ACCOUNT to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

Email us from the address on your account. Include the word "DELETE" and your username if you know it. We will verify you own the address, delete the account, and confirm by reply.

Email a deletion request

Or write to howkins.ops@gmail.com directly.

How long it takes

  • In-app deletion: immediate.
  • Email request: we action it within 7 days of verifying the request, and always within 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups containing your data roll off within 30 days of deletion.

Data on your device is separate. Much of the app works without an account, and that data lives only on your phone. Deleting your account does not erase it. To clear it, use Settings → Danger Zone → Clear All Data, or delete the app.

Just want to delete some of it?

You do not have to delete everything. Individual posts, messages, proofs, journal entries and workouts can be deleted one at a time inside the app, and your account stays.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Continue without an account" and nearly everything works — milestones, daily planning, the journal, fitness, CLEARDAY, exercises and games — stored on your device. Only the Accountability Zone, which is social by nature, needs a sign-in.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & All Data. It is immediate and irreversible. You can also request it without the app: see Delete Your Account and Data.

I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email

Check spam and promotions. The email opens the app directly. If it still hasn't arrived after ten minutes, email us and we will sort it manually.

How do I report someone, or block them?

Every post, comment, direct message and profile has a report button, with reasons and an optional description. Blocking is on any member's profile and is instant and two-way. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Full rules: Community Guidelines.

What is THE CORNER, and where does my message go?

THE CORNER is an AI chat inside CLEARDAY, powered by Anthropic's Claude. It asks for your permission by name before anything is sent, and it only sends what you type plus limited context — never your name, email, photos or journal. It is not a therapist, counselor or crisis line. Details: Privacy Policy, section 7. To withdraw consent, use Settings.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers and no purchases anywhere in the app. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Is my health and recovery data private?

Yes. Workouts, body measurements, journal entries and CLEARDAY recovery data are visible only to you, enforced at the database level. CLEARDAY urge-battle proof photos never leave your device at all.

Why is the app 18+?

Frequent strong language, cartoon violence, and frank adult discussion of substance and pornography use in the recovery module.

Something's broken

Email us with your device model, OS version, what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Health Disclaimer

Version 1.0 · Last updated: July 26, 2026

Milestone Mapping is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It is a self-directed education and training tool. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

What this app is

Milestone Mapping contains features that touch on health and wellbeing: fitness programs (THE IRON, Alpha Mode), a calorie and macro calculator, mood check-ins, self-development exercises for anger, stress and anxiety, and CLEARDAY, a self-directed 66-day program for people choosing to stop using cannabis and/or pornography.

All of it is education, structure and habit training that you run yourself. None of it is healthcare, therapy, counselling, medical treatment or a clinical program, and none of it is supervised by a licensed professional.

It is not a substitute for professional care

Nothing in the app is medical advice. Do not use it to diagnose yourself, and do not use it to decide whether to start, stop or change any treatment or medication. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you read or did in this app. If you are already in treatment, this app does not replace any part of it — tell your clinician you are using it.

About the research in the app

The app cites published research, and CLEARDAY includes day-by-day summaries of what studies have observed about recovery from cannabis use — sleep, memory, receptor density, lung cilia and so on. Those findings describe averages across groups of people under study conditions. They are not a prediction about you, not a guarantee, and not a clinical assessment of your progress. Individual experience varies enormously. Where evidence is weak or contested, the app says so.

Withdrawal can be dangerous

Stopping some substances suddenly can be life-threatening. Withdrawal from alcohol and benzodiazepines in particular can cause seizures and requires medical supervision. Opioid withdrawal, while rarely fatal, is far safer and more successful with medical support. If any of this applies to you, speak to a doctor before you change anything. CLEARDAY covers cannabis and pornography and is not designed for, and must not be used as, a detox protocol for any substance.

Before you exercise or change how you eat

  • Get clearance from a healthcare professional before starting any exercise program, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, a joint or back injury, are pregnant or postpartum, are over 40 and inactive, or take any medication.
  • Lifting weights carries a risk of injury. Work within your ability, learn the movement before you load it, and stop if something hurts.
  • Calorie and macro figures are estimates from a formula, not a prescription. They cannot account for your medical history, medication or metabolism.
  • If you have or have had an eating disorder, or a difficult relationship with food, weight or body image, do not use the nutrition and body-weight features. Speak to a professional instead. In the US, the National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is 1-866-662-1235.
  • Stop immediately and seek medical help if you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, fainting or sharp pain.

Mental health and emotional content

Shadow Work, the Anger Gym, Anxiety SOS and CLEARDAY ask you to look at difficult material — anger, shame, craving, relapse, past experiences. That can be uncomfortable and occasionally distressing. Go at your own pace, stop whenever you want, and work with a licensed therapist if the material is heavier than a self-help tool should carry. These exercises are not therapy and are not a treatment for any diagnosed condition.

THE CORNER is an AI

THE CORNER, the chat feature inside CLEARDAY, is an artificial intelligence. It is not a therapist, counselor, sponsor, doctor or crisis line. It can be wrong. It cannot assess your safety, and it must never be the only thing you reach for in a bad moment.

If you need help now

  • Immediate danger: call your local emergency number — 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 112 across the EU.
  • US — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988. 24/7, free, confidential.
  • US — SAMHSA National Helpline (substance use, mental health): 1-800-662-4357. 24/7, free, confidential, referrals to local treatment.
  • Canada — Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 988.
  • UK — Samaritans: 116 123.
  • Anywhere else: search "crisis line" plus your country, or go to your nearest emergency department.

Community Guidelines

Version 2.0 · Last updated: July 26, 2026

The Accountability Zone is a private social space inside Milestone Mapping where adults hold each other to commitments they made. These are the rules. Every member accepts them before they can enter, and they are enforced.

Zero tolerance. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours — including removing content and ejecting the people responsible.

The five house rules

🔥 Encourage, always

This is a place people come to become someone. Celebrate effort. Never mock a miss.

🚫 No shame, no interrogation

Nobody owes anyone an explanation for a missed day. "What's the plan for today?" is the only follow-up.

🛡️ Private by design

Only friends and squad-mates see your missions and proof. Never share someone else's content outside the Zone.

📷 Proof is yours

Post only your own photos and words. Nothing harmful, hateful or explicit — this is a locker room, not a battleground.

⚑ Report, don't retaliate

See something off? Every post, message and profile has a report button. Blocking is instant and two-way.

What is not allowed, ever

Do not post, send or upload content that is:

  • harassing, bullying, threatening or hateful toward any person or group, including on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age;
  • sexually explicit or exploitative — and absolutely nothing involving minors in any way. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the authorities and results in a permanent ban;
  • violent, or encouraging self-harm, suicide, eating disorders or harm to others;
  • promoting or facilitating the sale of drugs, weapons or other illegal goods;
  • spam, scams, phishing, impersonation or deliberately misleading;
  • infringing someone else's privacy, likeness or intellectual property, including posting a photo of a person who did not agree to it;
  • illegal, or promoting illegal activity.

Members recovering from substance or behaviour use share this space. Do not post content designed to trigger, taunt or bait someone's recovery.

How we enforce this

  • Automatic filtering. An objectionable-content filter runs both in the app and again inside the database, so blocked content cannot be posted even by a modified client. It catches hate slurs, child-exploitation terms and direct violent threats.
  • Reporting. Every post, comment, direct message, profile and vow carries a report button, with a reason and an optional description.
  • Blocking. Blocking is instant, two-way and enforced at the database level. A blocked member cannot see you, message you, or appear to you.
  • Review within 24 hours. Reports land in a moderation inbox that is checked daily. We remove content, warn, suspend or permanently ban depending on severity. Severe violations — child sexual abuse material, credible threats of violence — result in an immediate permanent ban with no warning.
  • Appeals. If you believe a decision was wrong, email us and we will look again.

Report something

Use the report button inside the app, or email howkins.ops@gmail.com with a description and, if you can, a screenshot. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Age

The Accountability Zone, like the rest of Milestone Mapping, is for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.

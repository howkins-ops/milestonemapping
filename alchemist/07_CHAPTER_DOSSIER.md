### Ch 7 — The Crystal Shop · *(the Merchant arc I — the inner sale)* · (T)
- **Coelho beat:** Broke after the robbery, Santiago climbs the hill to the dusty crystal shop and starts
  cleaning the glasses **before any deal exists**; two customers walk in while he works — "a good omen" —
  and the merchant hires him ("the Koran obliges me to feed the hungry"; fair commission, honest terms).
  Merchant teachings: **work before the ask**; hospitality/fairness as the foundation of trade.
- **Jon's testimony:** Mom's commission-only panic ("you're crazy, out of your mind") vs falling in love
  with commissions — a wage is a ceiling someone else builds; the "Super awesome!" greeting; the first-door
  ritual ("Hellllooo!! How are YOU?"); "people buy with emotion and justify with logic." *(F$CK THE 9-5:
  Sales — Commissions/Choose The Right Product/Choose The Right Organization; Enthusiasm.)*
- **Bigger lesson(s):** The first sale is always the **inner sale** — sell yourself on the trade, the
  product, and the org before you pitch anyone. Enthusiasm = *en-theos*, "the God within" — Tracy's "50% of
  any sale is a transfer of enthusiasm"; enthusiasm ≠ excitement (internal vs external — excitement dies in
  week two); tonality & body language carry 70–93% of the message; moderation — spice, not the meal.
- **Scene mini-arc:** (1) The hill, the dusty shop, the watching Merchant. (2) Polish first — the omen —
  the hire. (3) Commission as a door, not a ceiling. (4) En-theos: the fire that survives the rain.
  (5) Three unpolished glasses on the counter — the inner sale.
- **Exercise — Polish the Crystal** → `whatISell`, `whyIBelieveIt`, `enthusiasmSource`.
- **Framework:** *Lesson* — the work itself is the first pitch. *Principle* — half of any sale is
  transferred enthusiasm. *Trap* — mistaking week-one excitement for fire. *Opportunity* — close the inner
  sale and customers smell conviction instead of doubt. *Action* — name what you sell, why you believe it,
  where the fire lives. *Identity shift* — employee-minded → commission-hearted.
- **Dashboard:** Tests · Purpose 5 · Faith 4 · Fear 6 · Courage 5 · Trust 5.

### Ch 10 — The Merchant's Dream · *(the Merchant arc IV — the close; arc climax)* · (T)
- **Coelho beat:** The merchant names the **five obligations** of his faith — and admits he lives four. The
  fifth is Mecca: "I'm afraid that if my dream is realized, I'll have no reason to go on living… You want

- **Jon's testimony:** **No Means Know** ("shop around" = tell me about competitors; "price" = tell me more

  **value must exceed price** (the two-sided scale; value-stack, guarantees, reviews — never just drop the
  price; then ask or ASSUME the sale); Dad's ethics — "I could sell snow to an Eskimo… that doesn't mean
  you should." *(F$CK THE 9-5: Sales — No Means Know/Sympathy Vs Empathy/Why People Don't Buy/Value Must

  *Identity shift* — merchant-who-waits → closer-who-goes.

## The Business-mentor sales reservoir (deep teaching; PRIMARY HOME = the Crystal Shop arc Ch7–10; still echoes in Ch4/6/12/17)
- **Law of Probability:** track approaches → presentations → closes; ~97% "no" is the *math*, not a verdict.
- **Rapport — the Rock Collection:** "forget what I'm selling — tell me about these rocks" → she bought because
  he cared about *her*. Take a genuine interest first.
- **"No means Know":** objections = not enough info yet ("too expensive" = "tell me more about the value").
- **Listen to understand, not to reply** (Covey); tonality/body language is 70–93% of the message.
- **Empathy, not sympathy:** closers empathize and still close; sorry salespeople sympathize and lose.
- **Value must exceed price:** re-stack value; never just drop the price.
- **Enthusiasm = 50% of the sale** (en-theos, "God within"); "transfer of enthusiasm" (Tracy).
- **Mastery formula:** Knowledge + Practice = Skills; Skills + Time × Consistency = Mastery.
- **Choose the right product/org:** own what you sell; pick a place where *hundreds* win, not one or two.
- **Fill-Your-Cup:** can't pour from an empty cup; the Hour of Power; 5 ways to fill the cup → ties to the
  app's existing Daily / Fill Cup tabs.

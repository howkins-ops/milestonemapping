## 0 · THE THESIS (Jon's own frame, now backed by data)

Jon: *"If I wanted to quit weed I first have to have a belief that I am a person who has healthy lungs. Same thing with porn — who is the type of person I would have to start believing I am?"*

The research says he's right, precisely:

- **Identity precedes abstinence.** Recent quitters who self-identify as a NON-user are ~2.6× more likely to still be abstinent 3 months later (Tombor et al. 2015, n=574, prospective). ~80% of successful quitters adopt the new identity.
- **The killer label is "a user trying to quit."** It's the highest-relapse identity in the data (Callaghan et al. 2021, prospective, 544 quitters) — worse than keeping no label at all. Every quit app Jon has tried keeps him in exactly that identity: *a struggling user with a counter.*
- **Identity change in the FIRST TWO WEEKS predicts who makes it** (Beckwith/Best 2015). So identity work is Day-1 content, never "advanced" content.
- **Self-control is value-based choice** (Berkman 2017): the urge moment is won by whichever want is strongest *right then* — and identity is the cheapest, most durable way to raise the value of the clear choice at decision-time.
- Jon's own old-AI framework — the **Addict Mask vs. the Essence Self**, "that's not me, that's the Addict talking" — is externalization + defusion, and it's load-bearing in the design below. The Mask gets a name, a face, and a boss fight.

**The one-sentence product:** every other app counts the days you've survived; CLEARDAY builds the person who doesn't go back.

## 1 · RESEARCH COMPENDIUM → FEATURE MAP

Each finding below survives only because it becomes a feature. Effect sizes stated honestly; contested findings flagged.

### 1A · Identity engine science
| # | Finding (evidence) | Feature it becomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-user identity predicts staying quit; "trying to quit" predicts relapse (Tombor 2015; Callaghan 2021 — prospective, strong) | **The Label Ladder** — user picks his rung: "user trying to quit" → "someone who chose to stop" → "ex-user" → "clear." Ladder only ratchets UP, re-offered at milestones. App copy always speaks from his current rung. **Weekly Identity Pulse** (1 tap: which is truer right now?) is the app's REAL progress metric — a stuck pulse triggers identity work, because that answer *is* the relapse signal. |
| 2 | Self-perception: people infer who they are from watching their own behavior — strongest when self-view is uncertain, i.e. exactly an early quitter (Bem 1972; FITD d≈0.2–0.3, Burger 1999) | **The Ballot / evidence engine** — every action logs as a vote; after each rep the app closes the inference loop: *"Look at what you just did. Who does that?"* — user taps the answer himself. Never bribe reps with external rewards (kills the self-inference). |
| 3 | Labeling beats persuasion: "you ARE tidy people" outperformed "you should be tidy" (Miller/Brickman/Bolen 1975) | **Earned labels** — the app OBSERVES and LABELS after real logged behavior: "That's what a clear-headed man does. Because you are one." Never imperative shame copy ("stay strong!"). |
| 4 | Vivid FEARED self + hoped self move behavior, but ONLY when chained to concrete strategies (Markus & Nurius 1986; Oyserman RCTs — strong moderator) | **Two Doors** onboarding set-piece — user authors both selves in vivid specifics (Door A: me at 40 still hitting the pen / still in the tab at 2am; Door B: the clear one) and Door B won't seal until 3 concrete this-week actions attach to it. Door A resurfaces IN HIS OWN WORDS at high-risk moments. |
| 5 | Self-affirmation before threatening health info raises acceptance (2 meta-analyses, d≈0.3 — best-replicated small effect in the file) | **Affirm-then-inform law** — any hard truth screen (lungs, dopamine, dead mornings) is ALWAYS preceded by a 20-second values touch ("what matters most to you today?"). Never cold-open with scare content. |
| 6 | Induced hypocrisy: advocate publicly, then face your own gaps → behavior change (Priolo 2019 meta, r≈.35) | **Record the Case** — user records/writes a message persuading "someone exactly like you a year ago" why clear beats high, THEN privately lists his last 3 broken standards. His own case replays at risk windows: *"A message from you."* |
| 7 | Identity is social: relationships premised on the new identity drive behavior (Stryker; SIMOR — Best 2016) | **Witnesses stat** — track WHO knows ("I don't smoke anymore" told to a named person) as a first-class number. Weekly mission: grow the clear circle by one act. |
| 8 | One lapse carries "diagnostic utility" — it's news about who you are, which is why slips spiral (Bodner & Prelec; AVE — Marlatt) | **The slip-quarantine flow** (see §4) — attribution FIRST, ledger math second, immediate identity-consistent act third. |

### 1B · Language laws (every string in the app obeys these)
1. **The I-DON'T law** — refusals are always "I don't," never "I can't / I'm trying to stop" (Patrick & Hagtvedt 2012 — 4 studies incl. 10-day field; small-n, direction consistent). Input validation literally rejects hedge words ("trying," "less," "mostly") in the user's Law and explains why: *rules with exceptions force a fresh decision every time, and eventually lose one* (PRIME theory, West).
2. **The noun-direction law** — identity nouns ONLY for the desired self ("a clear one"), deployed at decision moments. NEVER a standing negative noun: no "addict," "relapser," "failure" anywhere in the app's voice (Kelly 2010: the label itself increases punitive/pessimistic judgment; Grubbs: for porn, the "addict" label is itself pathogenic). Lapses are verbs and events; identities are nouns. The one sanctioned negative noun is hypothetical + act-proximal: *"opening that tab would make you a man who breaks his own word"* (Bryan/Adams/Monin 2013 — "don't be a cheater" → near-zero cheating, replicated).
3. **The corner-man law** — in hot moments the app switches to second/third person, with the user's name when available (Kross 2014; Moser 2017: third-person self-talk regulates *without* cognitive effort — usable when the prefrontal cortex is offline; Furman 2020: works on food choice). Celebration returns to warm first person. *Distance for heat, closeness for wins.*
4. **The already-true law** — identity copy is present tense on today's evidence ("Day 12 of being someone who doesn't"), never aspirational-future, never struggler-framing.
5. **The receipts law** — anything shareable is a past-tense behavioral receipt ("30 days. Didn't touch it."), never an identity announcement (Gollwitzer 2009: identity claims made to an audience produce *premature completion* and LESS striving). Declarations are private and **produced** — spoken aloud or typed in full, never a checkbox (production effect, MacLeod: speaking > writing > typing > reading for encoding).

### 1D · Weed track vs porn track — the science says design them DIFFERENTLY
**THE 5 TRACK LAWS:**
1. **Body vs mind is the hero metric.** WEED leads with the day-indexed physiological recovery report (withdrawal is real, validated, and ENDS). PORN leads with values/defusion/shame — its physiology is contested, and over-promising destroys trust.
2. **Reward the clean day vs protect the slip.** Weed can lean into contingency-style celebration of clear days. PORN must never gamify streaks — the abstinence-violation effect on a broken porn streak triggers the binge (streak-reset shame is *the* documented porn-app failure).
3. **Withdrawal is physical vs psychological.** Weed: validated 2–4 week arc — sleep disruption + irritability first (day 1–3), peak day 2–6, vivid dreams = REM rebound = HEALING (day 2–10), sleep normalizes ~day 45. CB1 receptors renormalize in ~28 days; memory improves in week 1; the anhedonia valley (weeks 2–6) must be forecast BEFORE he hits it. Porn: the "flatline" (~day 14–45) is real-as-reported but psychological; name it so he doesn't quit during it; explicitly refuse to promise superpowers or an ED cure (anti-hype = trust differentiator).
4. **The cue is an object vs a context.** Weed: the pen is a loaded cue he CARRIES — environment mission = physically remove it ("you're not weak, you're carrying a loaded cue"). Porn: the cue is phone-in-bed + late night + alone + HALT-B (bored) — environment mission = phone curfew, out of the bedroom; and "just peeking" IS the relapse, intercept at the scroll.
5. **Neutral framing vs shame-proof framing.** Weed copy can be straight health/performance talk. Porn copy must be aggressively shame-proof: shame is the binge FUEL, not the brake (Grubbs PPMI, meta-analytic). All porn-track identity mechanics point at the positive pole only ("votes for who I'm becoming," never "votes against the addict I am").

### 1E · Why every app Jon tried failed — THE 7 SINS (never commit these)
1. **The Zeroing** — a counter reset is an in-app abstinence-violation trigger; the app itself tells the user to binge. NOTHING in CLEARDAY ever resets to zero.
2. **The Empty Counter** — a day count with no in-the-moment tool is a tombstone (I Am Sober's #1 complaint).
3. **The Paywalled Lifeline** — never gate the crisis tool; never let an update wipe progress (QUITTR's fatal trust burn).
4. **The Blocker Delusion** — blockers fight your hands, not your identity; they're disabled in Settings.
5. **The Groundhog Rep** — day 40 must not feel like day 2; celebration/copy variety engines required (Brainbuddy's churn driver).
6. **The Slot Machine** — never randomize WHETHER a rep pays; only the FORM of the celebration varies. No streak-anxiety notifications.
7. **The Outcome Trap** — reward the rep just done (can't be faked), not self-reported abstinence.

The two mechanisms with actual RCT wins in this category: **a genuinely useful crisis tool** (A-CHESS) and **rewarding verified actions** (reSET/contingency management, d≈0.42, immediacy matters). CLEARDAY's spine is exactly those two.

### 1F · THE JUICE LAWS (FX science — Jon loves special effects; here's how they serve the mission)
1. **<100ms detonation** — celebration fires the same frame the rep lands (reward-delay directly shrinks effect size). Sound + particles + (haptic) simultaneously.
2. **Juice the working machine** — FX on top of a psychologically sound exercise, never instead of one (Juice It or Lose It canon).
3. **Quiet during, loud after** — low FX density while he does the internal work (juice measurably degrades task performance mid-task — CHI 2024); 100% density at completion.
4. **Everything eases, nothing teleports** — squash-and-stretch, overshoot easing, 80–120ms hitstop on big moments.
5. **Guaranteed reward, surprising form** — every rep pays from a celebration deck (variants + rare "critical" celebrations). Predictable THAT, unpredictable WHAT.
6. **The vault only fills** — progress renders as owned physical objects (stamped votes, an evidence wall); nothing visually drains or shatters on a slip.
7. **Scale shine to stakes** — THE BIGGEST FIREWORKS IN THE ENTIRE APP fire on the first rep after a slip. The moment every other app punishes is the moment CLEARDAY throws the parade.

Plus: **goal-gradient + endowed progress** (Kivetz; Nunes & Drèze) — onboarding itself banks the first 3 votes so the bar is never empty; milestones spaced tighter early. Lally 2010 (the real "66 days" study): median 66 days to automaticity, range 18–254, and **missing a single day did not materially affect habit formation** — that citation is IN the app, shown on a missed day.

7. **The Whys** — multiple, each with an intensity rating; the hottest why is the one that surfaces mid-battle.

## 3 · THE URGE BATTLE (the centerpiece set-piece)

Full-screen cinematic takeover, launchable in ≤2 taps from ANYWHERE (topbar sun → big red-shifted URGE button; `mm:open-clearday` event from dashboard/SOS surfaces). Track picked on entry (or auto if single-track). The whole thing is real cognitive work with game FX — zero meditation framing. Target: 6–10 minutes, exit-early always allowed and still logged as a ride.

**ACT 0 · THE INTERRUPT** (5s) — screen cracks to black; his own IF-THEN fires: *"You planned for this exact moment."* Then the corner-man voice: *"You're rehearsing it right now — that rehearsal is the fuel. Cut it."* Hitstop, then the wave rises.
**ACT 1 · THE WAVE + THE MASK** (60–90s) — live wave canvas = the battle's spine. The Mask materializes (glitching dark shape) speaking HIS OWN logged lies. He counters each one by tapping the matching comeback from his coping cards — correct counters crack the Mask with shatter FX. Unanswered lies he must answer by TYPING a new comeback (which saves to his deck — the arsenal grows every battle).
**ACT 2 · THE STATIC STORM** (90s) — visuospatial mini-game while the peak passes (canvas: catch drifting light-orbs in a lens / rotate shapes to complete constellations — absorbing, pattern-based, escalating speed). The craving meter visibly drains as he plays. Science card on exit: *"That game just jammed the exact mental channel the craving runs on."*
**ACT 3 · NAME THE LINE** (45s) — defusion: type what the Mask is saying → app reframes: *"You are HAVING the thought that ___. You are not the thought."* → 30s+ drain-hold on the sentence, its power bar emptying, letters dissolving to static.
**ACT 4 · PLAY THE WHOLE TAPE** (60s) — two doors render cinematically in his own pre-written words: the relapse ending (color drains, sound dulls) then the clear ending (dawn palette floods in). Vividness prompts enforce the g=0.64 band: where are you, who's there, what does it feel like in your body.
**ACT 5 · THE WHY** (20s) — full-screen: his hottest why, his own words, huge type over the brightening horizon. If a photo/witness exists, it appears.
**ACT 6 · SPEAK THE LAW** (20s) — the seal: type (or speak) the Law IN FULL — "I don't hit the pen." No tap-through. Third-person option: *"[Name] doesn't do this anymore."*
**ACT 7 · THE VICTORY CEREMONY** (10s) — vote slams into the ballot: particle storm, screen shake, hitstop, dawn flash, sfx stack, +XP. Victory Ledger updates: *"Urges outlasted: 14. That confidence is the real weapon."*
**Optional branches:** BURN IT OFF (exercise beat, offered when he rates the urge ≥8/10) · ZOOM OUT (distancing interaction) · **the honest exit** — "It already happened" routes to the slip flow with zero shame.

## 4 · THE SLIP FLOW (where every other app kills its users)

Order is FIXED (anti-AVE, Marlatt):
1. **Attribution first** — before ANY self-statement: "What was the situation?" (place / person / feeling / time / trigger chips). Externalize-then-own.
2. **Ledger math** — "47 votes for, 3 against. You're winning this election by a landslide." NEVER Day 0. Clear days show as cumulative (47 of 50), never reset.
3. **One immediate identity-consistent act** — 60-second recovery vote (remove the cue / leave the room / text a witness / cold water) — logged with THE BIGGEST CELEBRATION IN THE APP (Juice Law 7).
4. **The Honest Ledger binary** — "Does last night change the Law, or break it once?" — blocks the silent rationalization drift where relapse actually lives (Meijer 2020). "Change the Law" opens a deliberate renegotiation flow with friction; "broke it once" re-arms.
5. Lally citation card on any missed day: *"The 66-day study: missing one day changed nothing. The data supports you."*
